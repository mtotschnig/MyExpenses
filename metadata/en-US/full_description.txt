<b>My Expenses</b> is your ultimate, privacy-friendly personal finance manager—giving you total control over daily expense tracking, budgeting, and investment portfolios with open-source transparency.

<b>Key Features:</b>

• <b>Daily Expense & Budget Tracking</b>: Effortlessly keep tabs on income and expenses on your phone or tablet. Create budgets, split transactions, and set up recurring transaction plans.
• <b>Investment & Portfolio Management</b>: Track stock, crypto, or custom asset portfolios, monitor price history, and manage multi-currency investments in real time.
• <b>Bank Integration & Reconciliation</b>: Connect directly to supported banks via FinTS or reconcile transactions manually against your bank statements for pixel-perfect balance tracking.
• <b>Smart Receipt Scanning (OCR)</b>: Automatically extract transaction details from photo receipts using advanced optical character recognition.
• <b>Web Interface for Desktop</b>: Access and manage your transactions from any web browser on your local network.
• <b>Powerful Analytics</b>: Visualize spending trends, distribution pie charts, and historical reports with customizable dynamic graphs.
• <b>Flexible Sync & Data Control</b>: Export and import data easily in QIF and CSV formats. Sync seamlessly across devices using WebDAV, Nextcloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive with client certificate support.
• <b>Privacy & Security First</b>: Open-source transparency, password or biometric lock screen security, and encrypted database protection.

<b>Unlock Premium Features:</b>

• <i>Contrib Key</i>: Highlights include unlocking unlimited accounts, unlimited recurring plans, PDF list exports, category distribution charts, and more.
• <i>Extended Key</i>: Includes all Contrib features, plus automated cloud synchronization, and advanced CSV import rules.
• <i>Professional Key</i>: Includes all Extended features, plus investment & portfolio tracking, history charts, budget monitoring, receipt OCR scanning, local web desktop interface, direct bank sync (Germany), and more.

<b>Permissions Transparency:</b>

• <i>Calendar</i>: Recurrent plans are saved to a dedicated local calendar or any calendar you assign.
• <i>Internet</i>: Anonymous usage tracking (opt-in), cloud synchronization and crash reports (opt-in).

Take control of your money and net worth today with <i>My Expenses</i>!
